# A Lion

*James Tate*

If a lion came up to me in the street

I would try to talk to him, say

A flower is nice, would you like to try one

Don't worry about me^[it is unclear on a first read who said this line, which make the poem great. here a potential violence built up from the lines before are elegantly resolved into empathy. just great]

Go home, all of you, he said

I will be alright
